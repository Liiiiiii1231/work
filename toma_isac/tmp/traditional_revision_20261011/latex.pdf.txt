E. Impact of System Load
   Fig. 4 investigates the average WSR under different system
loads by simultaneously varying the numbers of DL users, UL
users, and sensing targets, where K = J = L.
   It can be observed that the average WSR of all schemes de-
creases as K = J = L increases from 3 to 7. This degradation
is mainly caused by two factors. First, the available transmit
power, antenna elements, and spatial degrees of freedom
remain limited, while an increasing number of communication
users and sensing targets must share these resources. Second,
increasing the system load intensifies multiuser interference,
inter-target echo interference, and competition between com-
munication and sensing tasks.
